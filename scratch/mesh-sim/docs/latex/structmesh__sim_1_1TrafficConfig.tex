Flow-\/level traffic demand configuration. 

Three traffic models are supported, selected by {\ttfamily model\+:}

\endhead
{\ttfamily \char`\"{}constant\char`\"{}}   &All flows are active for the full simulation at {\ttfamily demand\+\_\+mbps}.    \\\cline{1-2}
{\ttfamily \char`\"{}poisson\char`\"{}}   &New flows arrive at {\ttfamily arrival\+\_\+rate\+\_\+hz} (network-\/wide Poisson process). Each flow has an exponentially distributed holding time when {\ttfamily holding\+\_\+time\+\_\+s} $>$ 0, otherwise it is permanent.    \\\cline{1-2}
